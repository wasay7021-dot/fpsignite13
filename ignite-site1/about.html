FPS IGNITE XIII

Ideas into action.

An entrepreneurial arena for the students at Foundation Public School and Headstart School System. IGNITE brings together creativity, strategy and teamwork — giving your delegation the opportunity to build a business from the ground up.

One team. Four perspectives.

Each delegation includes a Content Writer, Videographer, Techno-wiz and Spokesperson. One of these four members serves as Head Delegate. Delegations represent FPS and HSS Campuses.

The rules of the arena

  • Every delegation starts with 1,000 CR of event currency. CR has no real world monetary value.
  • Choose from ten industries and place your bid when the organizers open the auction.
  • The ten highest eligible bids per industry receive a place. Equal bids are ranked by submission time.
  • Winning bids are deducted at settlement. Pending bids do not reserve credits, so manage your commitments carefully.
  • Use your remaining credits to reserve a Green Screen Room, Office, Library or Lounge.
  • Your organizer manages event opening, closing, access and delegation details.
